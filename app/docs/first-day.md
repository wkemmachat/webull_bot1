# First-day checklist

1. **Sign in** and open the Dashboard. The eleven strategies should show a state (LONG, FLAT, 2x, trend, IN, OFF …) within a couple of minutes.
2. **Strategies → each strategy**: check the chart. Gold #1 shows the Supertrend line and the ADX panel; the regimes show
   both the signal index (QQQ or SMH) and the traded ETF (QLD or USD).
3. **Health check → Run check now**. Everything should be ok except perhaps the LINE and backup lines on day one.
4. **LINE alerts**: token, recipient, test message. Pick Instant / Digest / Off per event and your digest times.
5. **Execution**: the default is 10 lots × 10 minutes from 22:00 Bangkok for the metals; the regimes start at the market open with
   10 lots × 5 minutes. Change them if you like.
6. **Orders** during US hours: watch the first plans fill lot by lot.
7. Next morning: read the **digest** on LINE and the **Health check**.

When Paper looks right for a week, connect **Webull test**, then **Live** with a small amount: **Money → Allocate to bot**, then
**Give to strategies** (split equally or your own amounts).
